Overview & Device Support

  • Operating System: Ubuntu Touch 24 (Noble Numbat)
  • Base: Halium 11 (utilizing native Samsung vendor blobs for hardware acceleration)
  • Supported Models: Samsung Galaxy Tab A8 10.5 (2021) — SM-X200 (Wi-Fi), SM-X205 (LTE), and SM-X207

What Works & Known Issues

Working:

  • Display: Full 1200x1920 resolution, glitch-free
  • Touchscreen: Multi-touch support
  • GPU Acceleration: Mali Bifrost
  • Connectivity: Wi-Fi (2.4GHz & 5GHz) and Bluetooth
  • Audio: Speakers and 3.5mm headphone jack
  • Power & Peripherals: Battery reporting, charging, and USB OTG (mice, keyboards)
  • Cameras

Not Working / Untested:

  • LTE data on the SM-X207 variant
  • GPS

Prerequisites & Requirements

  • Unlocked bootloader
  • A PC running Windows (with Odin) or Linux (with Heimdall), plus working ADB / Fastboot drivers
  • Stock Samsung firmware (Android 11 or 12 base) for your exact device model
  • OrangeFox Recovery (or a compatible TWRP build for gta8 / gta8wifi)
  • vbmeta_disabled.tar (to disable Android Verified Boot)
  • The Ubuntu Touch build zip package (e.g., ubuntutouch-smx200.zip / ubuntu-touch-gta8.zip)

Installation Instructions

  • 1. Flash Stock Firmware:
    • Cleanly flash the matching stock Samsung firmware via Odin using BL, AP, CP (for LTE models), and standard CSC.
    Important: Do not use HOME_CSC. A clean flash ensures your vendor and dynamic partitions are intact.
  • 2. Flash Recovery & vbmeta:
    • Boot the tablet into Download Mode (hold Power + VOL-, then plug in the USB cable while holding VOL+ + VOL-).
    • In Odin, place OrangeFox Recovery in the AP slot.
    • Place vbmeta_disabled.tar in the USERDATA slot.
    • Under Odin's Options tab, uncheck Auto Reboot, then click Start.
    • Once flashing finishes, manually force-reboot into recovery by holding Power + VOL-; as soon as the screen goes black, quickly switch to holding Power + VOL+ until recovery launches.
  • 3. Format Data:
    • Inside OrangeFox / TWRP, go to Wipe → Format Data, type yes, and confirm.
    Do not skip this step: Stock encryption must be removed before sideloading Ubuntu Touch.
  • 4. Sideload Ubuntu Touch:
    • In recovery, open Menu → ADB & Sideload and swipe to start sideloading.
    • Connect your tablet to your PC and run:
    adb sideload ubuntutouch-smx200.zip
    Wait for the installation script to finish — it will create /data/ubuntu.img, unpack the rootfs, and bind the native vendor blobs.
